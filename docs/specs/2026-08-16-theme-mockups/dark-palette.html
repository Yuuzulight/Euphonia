Darkest — but not the gentlest
Saves power on OLED phones. The catch: pure black behind near-white text is the highest-contrast combination here, which tends to make text shimmer in a dim room — the opposite of "easier on the eyes."

My recommendation is A: it's the one that still looks like Euphonia, and it deliberately avoids both pure black and pure white so there's no glare at either end. Nothing here is locked — I can warm it up, cool it down, or push the accents brighter once you've picked a direction.
